FAQ

What sizes can you cut?
Letter height from 0.25″ up to 24″ tall. Total line width up to 50 feet (great for trailers and box trucks — contact us for anything wider).
Is this vinyl outdoor-rated?
Yes — all colors are outdoor-durable cut vinyl (Avery Dennison SC950-class), rated for several years outdoors depending on color and exposure.
Can I get a color that's not in stock?
Yes — mark it "special order" at checkout and we'll order that roll for your job. Adds a few business days versus our in-stock black.
Do you install it for me?
This storefront is for cut vinyl shipped/picked up for self-install (application instructions included). Need professional installation? Contact ICARUS ink directly for a wrap-shop quote.
